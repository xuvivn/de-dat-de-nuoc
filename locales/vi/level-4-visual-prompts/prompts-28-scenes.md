# Đặc Tả Phong Cách & Đề Bài Vẽ 28 Tranh Gợi Nhớ Theo Từng Chương (Level 4)

Tài liệu này lưu chuẩn mực mỹ thuật thống nhất cho toàn bộ 28 bức tranh gợi nhớ của *Đẻ đất đẻ nước*, mỗi Chương một tranh.

> **Trạng thái:** bộ đề bài đã hoàn tất; **chưa tạo ảnh**. Khi vẽ xong, đặt tệp vào `assets/images/` đúng tên trong [illustration-gallery.md](illustration-gallery.md) và bật `image_generated` cho tranh đó trong `story-index.json`.

## Phong Cách Vẽ Thống Nhất

- **Chất liệu mô phỏng:** Than chì đen (*charcoal*) kết hợp phấn tiên màu chàm sâu (*deep indigo*) và đất nung ấm (*warm terracotta ochre*) trên giấy mỹ thuật ngà, vân thô nhẹ.
- **Tinh thần nét vẽ:** Phác họa bằng tay, mộc mạc, hơi nhòe tự nhiên; mảng tối bằng than, mảng ấm bằng phấn đất; viền ngoài phai mềm vào nền giấy trắng.
- **Không khí:** Sử thi dân gian Mường — tĩnh, có chiều sâu, giàu khoảng thở; không chói, không bóng bẩy kỹ thuật số.
- **Tuyệt đối không:** chữ viết, chữ số, khung viền cứng, chi tiết kỹ thuật số, màu huỳnh quang.
- **Khuôn hình:** khổ ngang 4:3, bố cục mở, chừa nhiều giấy trắng quanh chủ thể.
- **Nhân vật:** nhỏ so với thiên nhiên; khuôn mặt đơn sơ, không tả chi tiết giải phẫu.
- **Nội dung nhạy cảm:** kể bằng **dấu vết và bóng** — không tả máu, không tả xác, không tả bạo lực trực diện.

### Câu lệnh phong cách dùng lặp lại

> *Hand-drawn charcoal and chalk pastel illustration on textured ivory paper; palette limited to deep charcoal black, indigo blue, warm terracotta ochre and soft chalk white; grainy soft edges fading into the paper; quiet Vietnamese epic atmosphere; wide 4:3 composition with generous margins; strictly no text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no digital gloss.*

Indigo water, charcoal mud, a single warm ochre light on the horizon.

- **Tranh 03 (Chương 3) — `tranh-03-de-nuoc.png`:** *[same style]* A cracked, drought-stricken plain under a bleached sky; an old man sits mending a fishing net at the window of a small stilt house, looking up as enormous storm clouds gather over the hills; the first heavy raindrops fall as bright chalk streaks, and far below a dry riverbed begins to darken with water. Terracotta dust, indigo storm, one small warm lamp in the house.

- **Tranh 04 (Chương 4) — `tranh-04-de-cay-si.png`:** *[same style]* An immense banyan-like sacred tree growing from fresh dark soil, its trunk wider than a house, four great roots sinking and thirty branches spreading; the highest branch pierces a low ceiling of cloud. - **Tranh 05 (Chương 5) — `tranh-05-de-muong.png`:** *[same style]* The sacred tree falling across a vast valley at dusk; its enormous trunk splits into hundreds of branches, and where each branch strikes the earth a small valley opens with a cluster of stilt roofs and a thread of smoke. - **Tranh 06 (Chương 6) — `tranh-06-de-nguoi.png`:** *[same style]* From the mossy, broken trunk of the fallen sacred tree, a serene elderly woman emerges as if grown from the wood, her hair like bark, her hands cradling two pale eggs; beside her two small boys stand in wonder, and above them a pair of tiny birds weave grass and silk into a hanging nest. Soft ochre glow on the trunk, deep indigo forest behind.

- **Tranh 07 (Chương 7) — `tranh-07-chia-nam-chia-thang.png`:** *[same style]* At dawn on a wide river, a rooster stands on the back of a calm duck floating among lily pads, its beak lifted as the sun rises over the hills; on the bank, an elder carves twelve notches into a tall wooden calendar post while villagers watch, and a thin crescent moon fades above. Warm ochre sunrise, indigo water, chalk-white mist.

### Hồi II — Lập Lang Dựng Mường Và Sáng Tạo Văn Hóa

- **Tranh 08 (Chương 8) — `tranh-08-dit-dang.png`:** *[same style]* A crowd of villagers carrying covered trays of betel and green tea arrives at the open veranda of a large stilt house; a dignified man in fine dark clothing sits on a woven mat, one hand lifted as if naming a price, while at the foot of the three entrance steps a suggestion of shadowy horned shapes waits in the indigo dusk. Restrained, no monsters in detail — only silhouettes and glowing eyes.

- **Tranh 09 (Chương 9) — `tranh-09-lang-ta-cai.png`:** *[same style]* The same village procession before a second, grander stilt house; a man in a long dark robe stands on the steps with a silver sword half-drawn, facing a great serpentine shadow coiling around the gate posts, its scales suggested by charcoal hatching rather than shown clearly. Villagers wait behind him in a silent line. Cold indigo night, one ochre torch.

- **Tranh 10 (Chương 10) — `tranh-10-lang-cun-can.png`:** *[same style]* A tall, broad-shouldered man in a seven-span loincloth and nine-span robe stands alone in the middle of a village road at dusk, sword raised but not striking; around him, shadowy spirits and a long dragon-shape flee into the dark forest, dissolving into charcoal strokes. Behind him, villagers watch from doorways. Strong terracotta light on his figure, indigo night.

- **Tranh 11 (Chương 11) — `tranh-11-lam-nha-lang-cun-can.png`:** *[same style]* A community raising a long stilt house: men lift heavy timber posts, women pass bundles of thatch, an elder marks the floor plan in the shape of a turtle shell; in the foreground a hunter kneels beside a calm golden turtle, listening. Warm ochre timber, charcoal shadows, indigo forest edge.

- **Tranh 12 (Chương 12) — `tranh-12-tim-lua-tim-nuoc.png`:** *[same style]* Before the mouth of a glowing cave, a small beetle and a hovering gadfly approach a seated fire spirit whose hands hold a flame and a gourd of water; around them lie scattered bundles of fire and clay jars, with wisps of steam and sparks rising. The figures are tiny against the cave. Ochre firelight, deep indigo rock.

- **Tranh 13 (Chương 13) — `tranh-13-tim-com-tim-lua.png`:** *[same style]* A woman carrying two woven baskets of forest roots and cakes climbs a narrow mountain path toward a serene rice goddess standing among terraced fields; below her, the valley is a soft pattern of indigo water and ochre earth, and a few grains of rice glow like small stars in her open palm. Chalk-white mist between the terraces.

- **Tranh 14 (Chương 14) — `tranh-14-tim-ruou.png`:** *[same style]* At a festive stilt house, two barefoot boys sit on a mat beside a row of tall clay wine jars; an elder spreads roots, leaves and bark before them, showing how to shape yeast cakes, while musicians and laughing guests fill the background. Warm ochre lamplight, indigo night beyond the open wall.

- **Tranh 15 (Chương 15) — `tranh-15-tim-lon-tim-ga.png`:** *[same style]* A woman with a basket of round sweet cakes stands at the gate of a heavenly farmyard, greeted by two old women surrounded by fat pigs and a flock of chickens with bright combs; a small piglet escapes between the fence stakes. Soft ochre and chalk-white animals, indigo sky, distant clouds like hills.

- **Tranh 16 (Chương 16) — `tranh-16-tim-trau.png`:** *[same style]* On a quiet hillside at dusk, a mother buffalo and a mother tiger stand facing away from each other, each with her young beside her; between them a broken woven fence and a trail of footprints going in two directions. No violence, only distance and sorrow. Charcoal silhouettes, indigo sky, a thin ochre line of sunset.

- **Tranh 17 (Chương 17) — `tranh-17-lang-cun-can-lay-vo.png`:** *[same style]* Beside a stone well at the edge of a village, a man in a long dark robe stands watching a young woman comb her long hair; behind him, villagers stand in a tight, disapproving row, and far above the hills a storm cloud gathers with a single fork of chalk-white lightning. Restrained and quiet, no confrontation.

- **Tranh 18 (Chương 18) — `tranh-18-lay-vo-khac-cho-lang-cun-can.png`:** *[same style]* A wedding procession winds along a village path: bearers carry covered trays of betel and rice, a bride walks beneath a small canopy, an old matchmaker with a cheerful crooked smile leads the way, and children throw small fruits from the roadside. Warm ochre and chalk-white, indigo morning shadows.

- **Tranh 19 (Chương 19) — `tranh-19-de-trong-dong-va-chia-dat.png`:** *[same style]* Inside a smoky foundry, workmen pour molten bronze into a great clay mold while an elder watches; in the foreground a finished bronze drum rests on the ground, its face marked with a radiating star and flying birds; beyond the open shed, a father divides land among four grown sons, one receiving a tiny patch no bigger than a monkey's ear.

### Hồi IV — Chu Kỳ Cây Chu

- **Tranh 20 (Chương 20) — `tranh-20-tim-chu.png`:** *[same style]* In a moonlit forest clearing, a humble man kneels with a crossbow lowered before a radiant tree — its trunk like stone, its leaves like bronze, its flowers like gold and tin; a small bright bird perches above him, and around the tree stand a tiger, a slow loris, a deer and a peacock in quiet attendance. In the far shadow, the bulky silhouette of a forest ape. Ochre glow, deep indigo night.

- **Tranh 21 (Chương 21) — `tranh-21-chat-chu.png`:** *[same style]* A colossal sacred tree leans and begins to fall across a valley; hundreds of tiny people pull long ropes from the ground, while in the foreground a single small hand-axe rests on a stump, carefully placed. Far below, a dark river waits. Dust and chalk-white mist rise. No bodies, no blood — only the enormous tree and the small axe.

- **Tranh 22 (Chương 22) — `tranh-22-lam-nha-chu.png`:** *[same style]* A vast, many-roomed stilt longhouse rises at night, its long ridge beam curving like a boat, dozens of windows glowing warm; builders climb ladders with tools, and in the courtyard a golden turtle rests in a shallow basin of luminous water, casting soft light across the walls. Ochre windows, indigo night, chalk stars.

- **Tranh 23 (Chương 23) — `tranh-23-dot-nha-chu.png`:** *[same style]* The great longhouse burning at night, flames drawn as broad ochre and chalk strokes, smoke covering the moon; on the roof, two small cats and two monkeys run with tiny fires tied to their tails, and in the foreground two children stand hand in hand, seen only from behind, watching. No injury, no bodies — grief and fire only.

### Hồi V — Trừ Thú Dữ Và Dẹp Loạn Giặc Ma

- **Tranh 24 (Chương 24) — `tranh-24-san-moong-lo.png`:** *[same style]* An enormous beast lies across two hills, its back like a ridge, its eyes two red-ochre points, its tail a fallen tree; below, tiny hunters stretch a long net across a valley and raise spears, while a line of villagers with drums climbs the slope. The beast is mostly silhouette, immense and quiet.

- **Tranh 25 (Chương 25) — `tranh-25-san-ca-dien-san-qua-dien.png`:** *[same style]* A river churning with dark shapes of leaping fish as three men in a small boat strike with long swords; on the muddy bank a single great fish lies still, and above it the sky fills with a spiral of crows, one bird in the center carrying a bright chalk-white glint in its beak. Indigo water, charcoal birds, ochre mud.

- **Tranh 26 (Chương 26) — `tranh-26-giac-ma-ruong.png`:** *[same style]* A ghostly army advances across ripening rice fields at dusk: figures with red heads and black heads, banners made of leaves and wind, spears like reeds; on a tall bamboo above the village a lone figure sits with a long sword across his knees, looking down. The army is drawn as layered translucent charcoal strokes, more suggestion than detail.

- **Tranh 27 (Chương 27) — `tranh-27-giac-ma-may-giac-ma-lang.png`:** *[same style]* A flooded river plain under a low storm sky; from the water rises a tall dragon-man with a red crest and a serpent's patterned body, one hand lifting a sword; on the bank a warrior in a red sash stands firm, sword raised. High above, a single crow flies toward distant cave-mouths carrying a white bone. Indigo flood, ochre crest, chalk bone.

### Hồi VI — Khải Hoàn

Warm ochre sunrise, long indigo shadows, chalk-white mist.
