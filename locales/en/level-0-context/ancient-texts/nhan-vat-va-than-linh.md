# Typical Characters, Gods and Things

> **Material type:** List of characters
> **Use when:** before telling a new Episode; Re-examine the relationships between characters
> **Source:** *Appendix A.2* of the consolidated version

---

## 1. The gods of origin

- **Mr. Thu Tha - Mrs. Thu Thien:** Two primordial gods were born from two branches of a ninety-branched green tree after a great flood, opened the order of all things and ordered twelve months of the year.
- **Mr. Pong Peu:** The water god sits and weaves a fishing net, praying for heavy rain to create rivers and seas (*Giving water*).
- **King of Heaven:** The highest position in the heavens; Sometimes jealous, sometimes afraid, sometimes having to negotiate with the world. He is the father of three girls, A Sao - A Sang - A Rang.
- **Thunder God - Lightning Goddess (Thunder Cun - Lightning Lady):** The two descended to earth during a thunderstorm; The skillful Lightning Lady in Chapter 17.
- **Ta Cam Cot:** The fire god keeps fire and water; where Vi Linh and Tun Mun came to ask (Chapter 12).

## 2. The line that gave birth to humanity

- **Da Dan Lady:** The goddess reincarnated from the trunk of a rotten tree branch, gave birth to Silver Butterfly Cun and Bo Butterfly Cun, taught Tung Birds - Gaur Birds to weave silk to make nests, organized the incubation of Chieng eggs and later sewed brocade clothes for the king; In folk history, it is also associated with the story *The Snake's Son*.
- **Cun Butterfly Silver - Cun Butterfly Bo:** The two brothers got married to two fairies who were the children of the King of Heaven (*Nam A*, *Nang Hai*), forming two large lineages **Lang family** and **Dan family**, giving birth to ten children, the youngest being the pair **Thong Chim Tung male - Gaot female**.
- **Tao Chao Bird - Skylark:** The tiny pair of birds incubated *Po eggs and Chieng eggs* for nine months by using golden and silver wings to shade the eggs so that the eggs hatched into hundreds of nations and the Lang brothers.

## 3. The first three Langs and the Lang family

- **Dit Dang, Lang Ta Cai, Lang Cun Can:** The first three brothers hatched from Chieng's eggs; The first two brothers could not overcome the evil spells that blocked their path, but **Lang Cun Can** bravely subdued the evil spirits, became the Lang who built houses, found fire, rice, wine, buffalos, cast bronze drums and was the father of the next Langs.
- **Va Hai Kip** *(also spelled Va Hai Chieng, Va Hai Kit)*: Lang Cun Can's younger sister; The illegal marriage between two brothers gave birth to twelve Demon boys, causing great disaster.
- **Lang Cun Can's four wives and four children:**
  - **Bang Dau Dau** *(son of King Binh Lac)* gave birth to **Cun Toi**;
  - **Vat Dau Dau** *(son of King Vinh Vuong)* gave birth to **Cun Tang**;
  - **A Sao, A Sang, A Rang** *(son of the Heavenly King)* gave birth to **Lang Cun Khuong**;
  - **Miss Ut** *(raised girl in the village)* gave birth to **Toong Ín**.

## 4. Those who learn the profession, keep the profession

- **Ms. Bui Rut:** The brave hunter caught black and yellow turtles and learned how to build nine-compartment Muong stilt houses simulating the shape of turtle shells.
- **Visiting Cu Linh and Tun Mun:** *Visiting Cu Linh* is a scarab beetle that went to ask for fire from the fire god **Ta Cam Cot** but carelessly let the irrigation water turn off in the middle of the road so he was punished to live with dung; *Tun Mun* (along with Mun Mach, Mun Mong — horse flies) wisely peeked through the basket's slats and learned the trick of pulling the tinder to light a fire.
- **Lady Dat Cai Đình:** A skillful, kind-hearted woman of the Muong village (*good and wise child*), many times she took on the responsibility of going to heaven to meet **the Rice Fairy** to ask for rice seeds, to the house of **Mrs. Hung - Mrs. La** to ask for pig and chicken breeds, and to Khanh Hao land to learn the profession of growing mulberries, raising silkworms, and weaving brocade.
- **Pap boy - Pham boy and Mr. Lang Kham Dam** *(heterophone: La Khan Dan)*: Two buffalo herding boys got lost in Lang Kham Dam's family's fun festival and learned the secret to making wild leaf wine yeast.
- **Mother Da Dan (role of dressmaker):** Later, she was welcomed to cut loose pants and scarves, sew three-seven pants, green brocade belts, and chicken-crested silk scarves for the king (Chapter 28).

## 5. Those who suffered injustice

- **Tam Tach and his two children (Tam, Tach's son):** The kind and loyal servant found the spirit tree; was tricked into marrying by Lang Cun Khuong *cough chu* and then killed to use the bones to pull the tree. The two children took revenge and burned down the chu house. The blood flowed and turned into a **Giant Moon**.
- **Khan Dong - Miss Song Don:** The couple was forced by Lang to find the golden turtle; Thanks to the brindle dog to announce the news and his touching cry, **Buddha** saved him from the jaws of stone.
- **Toong In:** Lang Cun Can's youngest son. To live is to lie and wage war; When he died, he became a ghost guarding the fields, then he became a ghost who lived under Thuy Palace. The most important unjust character in Episode V.

## 6. Creatures and treasures

- **Si tree:** The tree connecting heaven and earth; When it falls, each branch becomes a branch.
- **Chu tree (dying tree):** The sacred tree of the family and community authority (*stone chu tree, bronze chu tree, brass flower, tin fruit*).
- **Monong Lo:** Beast born from the blood of Tam Tach and Tach under the si tree; grew up through ten names.
- **Herd of black otters and silver otters:** Pull up the chu tree from Van Chieng; I only asked to tear that straw and give birth at Lang's house.
- **Yellow Bird:** Gave Tam Tach fruit and told him not to tell the king Ke Cho.
- **Van Dog:** The dog led Song Don to save Khan Dong.
- **Crow:** Take the cactus back to Hang Hao Drum Cave; be rewarded by the King of Heaven.
- **Dark Tortoise - Golden Tortoise:** Two turtles taught us twice how to build a house (the first time the materials were wrong, the next time they were correct).
- **Buffalo:** The animal must choose to live with people or with the forest (Chapter 16).
