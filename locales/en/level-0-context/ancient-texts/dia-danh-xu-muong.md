# Places of Muong Land

> **Type of document:** List of place names
> **Used when:** telling Act I (Laying the Muong), Act IV (Chapter 21 pulling cycle), Act VI (the way to Dong Chi)
> **Source:** *Appendix A.3* of the consolidated version

---

## 1. Ancient Muong region of Thanh Hoa - Hoa Binh

Most of the places in *Laying Muong* and the journey to pull the chu tree are in the two provinces of **Thanh Hoa** and **Hoa Binh**.

> **Note to the narrator:** There is no need to know the exact commune. Just need to know the **direction**: from the Ba Thuoc hills (Thanh Hoa) follow the Ma River down, then turn to the Hoa Binh villages, and finally return to Dong Chi - Ke Cho.

---

## 2. Two places "Chu Hill"

This is the easiest place to get confused, because the epic has **two different Chu hills**:

1. **Chu Hill in Chapters 4–5** (where the top of the sycamore tree broke down): belongs to **Cam Chau** and **Cam Tan** communes, **Cam Thuy** district, **Thanh Hoa** province.
2. **Chu Hill in Chapters 20–21** (where stone chu trees and dong chu leaves grow): located on the hills of **Ri Lay, Lai Lang** (Lai Ly, Lai Lang) in **Ba Thuoc** district, **Thanh Hoa** province.

> When telling, you can call the first one **the Chu hill of the sycamore tree**, the second one the **The Chu hill of the sycamore tree** — children won't get confused.

## 3. Chu tree pulling route along Ma River to Muong Hoa Binh land (Chapter 21)

### Ba Thuoc area (Thanh Hoa)
Lai Ro, Lai Re wharf (Thiet Ong commune), Ho Do - Ho Vanh waterfall, Ken village, Bua Mu, Cam wharf, Nan mountain (Lan Han, Long Van commune), Lan Ai mountain, De village, Xa mine, Muong Khoong, Muong Lam (Chieng Lam, Dien Lu commune), Dat village (Quy Luong commune), Can school.

### Cam Thuy & Thach Thanh area (Thanh Hoa)
Muong Vong, Khoong truong, Bai Khai (Cam Tu, Cam Thuy), Cot wharf (Ma river, Cam Son), Dong Kho, Tai Can, Quan Ven (Kim Tan), A cave, Uong stream, Cat truong (Cat street), Khuong truong, Dinh Trong (adjacent to Thach Thanh - Ha Trung), Pu Be (adjacent to Thach Thanh - Lac Son).

### Lac Son, Tan Lac, Da Bac (Hoa Binh)
Kheng Mountain - Cai Mountain (Van Duc, Lac Son), Muong Tre, Muong Coc (Thuong Coc, Lac Son), Dao Quang Doc (Tan Lac), Ben Peng - Ben Pung (Ngoc My, Tan Lac), Muong Rong (Phuc Tuy, Lac Son), Muong Khai - Muong Be - Muong Chin (Phu Luong, Chi Dao, Lac Son), along with Muong Vong, Vo, Tram, Tro, Vin Vong, Khoi River, Muong Mac, Muong Qua, Muong Truong, Muong Um, Muong Moc, Muong Vang, Muong Cua.

### Kem and Khen mountains (Chapter 24)
That means **Mount Xem** and **Mountain Khen** in **Tan Lac** district, **Hoa Binh province** - where Moong Lo was crushed by rocks and killed.

### Ba Vi – Ke Cho Region (Chapters 21, 28)
Mount Tien, Muong Quyen, Muong Lang (in the direction of Ba Vi area, old Ha Tay) lead to the capital **Dong Chi Tam Quan Ke Cho**.

## 4. Sacred and legendary places

- **Drum Cave, Hao Cave:** Sacred caves at Muong Troi level where humans were born; This is also the place where crows bring back cacti (Chapter 27).
- **Ai River, Am River:** Legendary rivers associated with the realm of Am, the underworld.
- **Ang Van Chieng (Ven Chieng):** The abyss of water where the chu tree sank (Chapter 21).
- **Bai Hill, Ri Lay Hill, Lai Lang:** Key hill areas of Acts I and Chapters IV–V.
- **Tam Quan Ke Cho Bronze Lead:** Lang Dynasty capital, destination of the final procession.

---

## 5. How to read a list of place names when narrating

The list of places in Chapter 21 is very long. If you read each name slowly, your child will lose their pulse. How to keep the rhythm:

- **Read in clusters of 3–5 names**, one breath per cluster, with very short breaks between clusters.
- **Do not click on each name.** Only click on names with strong images: *Ho Do - Ho Vanh waterfall*, *Lan Ai mountain*, *Cot wharf*, *Pú Be*, *Muong Vang*.
- **Use a leading sentence:** *"The chu tree passes through how many villages, how many wharves - in every village you can hear its voice."*
- **End the list with a slow beat:** *"...then return to Dong Chi."*

> The list is not for remembering everything. It's to create a **feeling of going away** — and for listeners in the village to recognize the name of their land.
