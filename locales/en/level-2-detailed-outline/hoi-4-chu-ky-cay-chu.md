# Level 2 (Detailed Outline) — Act IV: The Tree Cycle — Find, Chop, Build, and Burn

> **Level goal:** Detailed outline of the 4 chapters (20–23) of *Giving Birth to Earth*, for storytellers 9–10 years old. This is the longest and heaviest episode: a sacred tree is found, cut down, dragged back, built a house, then burned — and from the ashes a monster is born.
