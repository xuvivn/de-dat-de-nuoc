# Level 2 (Detailed Outline) — Act I: Opening Heaven and Earth and Origin of Race

> **Level goal:** Detailed outline of the first 7 chapters of *Giving Birth to Earth*, for storytellers 9–10 years old. Each chapter maintains the title and flow of the full text, but is divided into three blocks: characters/images, developments, and narrative highlights.

---

### Chapter 1: Prologue — Telling an old story
- **Central Characters & Images:** The storyteller (the priest/village elder), the silver butterfly, the dragonfly, the Milky Way River, Dong Bong Hill, the dragon yard, the world is still empty.
- **Detailed developments:**
 - The invitation to listen to the story resounds in four directions: on the hill to talk to silver butterflies, in the water to talk to dusk kha, in the sky to talk to the Ngan River, in the old people's house to pass it on to children and grandchildren.
 - The three stanzas "Wanting..." refer to the way of life of the ancient Muong people: if they want silk, they go to the hills to plant mulberries, if they want fish, they go down to the river and cast their nets, and if they want to listen to stories, they sit back. Stories help people forget hunger and fatigue.
 - A picture of nothingness: there is no land on earth, there is no sky in the sky, there are no stars, grass, roads, hills; The land is still loose, the water is still loose.
 - Everything "wants to wake up" but has nothing to wake it up: hooks don't have hooks yet, baskets don't have dust yet, swords don't have steel yet, carrying rice doesn't yet have wine, swallows don't have birds yet.
 - Humans don't have a face yet; Brother, brother, and sister still fight over the religion of the king; There are no stupid or talented people. There is no old man in the house who transmits messages back and forth.
- **Significance / Storytelling highlight:** This chapter is the gateway to the epic: it does not tell the event but creates a space for everything that follows to be born. The narrative voice must be the voice of the elderly in the house, not the voice of explanation.

### Chapter 2: Laying ground
- **Central Characters & Images:** The great flood, the green tree with ninety branches, Mr. Thu Tha, Ms. Thu Thien, the first of all species.
- **Detailed developments:**
 - One year of heavy rain, water crossed seven U hills and flooded nine Bai hills; fifty days the water recedes, seventy days the water recedes, leaving the ground first.
 - From moist soil grows a green tree with ninety branches, branches reaching up to the sky, and a trunk that can vibrate; In the canopy there were the voices of women and girls.
 - The first two branches turned into Mr. Thu Tha and Mrs. Thu Thien, becoming a good couple, giving life to chickens with spurs, melons that can climb, bamboos that have thorns, and humans that can speak.
 - The land is no longer fragmented, the water is no longer turbulent, the sky is no longer vague; There is a way down and a way up. Each "already exists" sentence opens up: hooks have slang, swords have steel, birds have friends, people have brothers.
 - Underwater gives birth to fish, crabs, shrimps, otters, and dragons; On land, there are Bai hills, young bamboo shoots, and pennywort amaranth. But there is still no one in the house to communicate.
- **Meaning / Storytelling highlight:** This is the chapter "giving birth to the land" in the true sense: the land is formed after water, then from the land gives birth to trees, from trees gives birth to two ancestors. The way of telling should be like opening each door, not like listing.

### Chapter 3: Giving birth to water
- **Central Characters & Images:** Mr. Pong Peu, drought of nine months and twelve years, heavy rain, God of Thunder, goddess of Lightning.
- **Detailed developments:**
 - Opens with a dry scene: roosters crow on the mountain pass, crows cry on the mountains, the sun is shining but the harvest doesn't produce rice, the fishing nets don't produce fish. The soil is fibrous, the cassava vines are withered, the cassava forest does not grow bamboo shoots.
 - Nine months of drought, twelve years of sunshine: dried up streams, broken turtle shells, broken deer antlers, buffaloes eating frozen soil, people drinking dew water.
 - Mr. Pong Peu sat at the window of the wing, weaving nets inside, looking out to the land, looking up to the sky, wishing for rain.
 - The sky was filled with clouds, the wind was on all four sides, the God of Thunder came down to scream, the goddess of Lightning came down to scream. The first rain at night is the size of an eggplant seed, the morning rain is the size of a grapefruit, the rain nine nights and nine days, breaks branches and leaves, washes deer antlers, washes wild bird wings.
 - Four months low tide, seven months low tide; Nine hills flow into one stream, nine streams flow into one river, nine rivers flow into one pool. Water loosens black soil, yellow soil; drifting crabs, fish, turtles, turtles, and tadpoles out to sea.
- **Meaning / Narrative highlight:** Water comes after thirst, not to punish but to save. This chapter teaches rhythm: dry – wish – rain – withdraw; The narrator must maintain the relief at the end.

### Chapter 4: Giving birth to a sycamore tree
- **Central Characters & Images:** The fast growing sycamore tree, the Heavenly King and his father and son the Heavenly King, the Hoc worm, the Gang worm, Chu hill.
- **Detailed developments:**
 - Water-soaked valley and hill soils have become loose; The sycamore tree grows profusely and is as big as a tree: at night it's as big as a pestle, in the morning it's as big as a mat, and in the afternoon it's as big as a four-foot-three tree.
 - Si has four submerged branches, thirty floating branches, one towering branch soaring straight into the sky, covering one side of the land and the horizon.
 - The King of Heaven sat at the window drinking wine, looking down he saw his delusion; In the world, drying rice does not dry, drying vegetables does not dry. It's so annoying that I want to destroy it, but I'm afraid it will damage the seedling fields and potato fields and people will starve to death.
 - Heaven sent the Hoc worm to eat the outside of the skin, and the Gang worm to carve out the core and eat the heart of the sycamore tree for sweetness. The worm's hollow body is as long as a palm leaf; Gang worms have shaggy fur, yellow leopard stripes, and their tips swoop down in the middle of the night.
 - Sycamore trees wither, yellow, rotten roots, fallen stumps; The fallen branches filled the valley, the broken head fell on Chu hill. The story changes to "the original si long tree, rotten branches" to give birth to Muong Nuoc.
- **Meaning / Storytelling highlight:** The sycamore tree is the axis of the universe: it connects heaven and earth, and its death is the death of reproduction — from its body springs water, water, and all living things. It should not be seen as an accident, but as a transformation.

### Chapter 5: Giving birth
- **Central Character & Image:** Snake Ly Ky Lai Cai, silver-headed bee, fallen tree root with one thousand nine hundred and nineteen branches, first villages, young green branches demanding to return to the Land of Thunder.
- **Detailed developments:**
 - A dead sycamore tree is a great omen. The body of ignorance turns into the snake Ly Ky Lai Cai, with nine arms and twelve heads, the tail is at the navel of the mountain, the head is at Chu hill; When a snake bites, it bleeds black blood, and the black blood turns into snakes.
 - The protruding eyes turn into silver-headed bees, the sting is nine spans long, the back is nine fathoms long; Bees hunt deer, weasels, monkeys, and even chase tigers. Crushed leaves turn into ferocious beasts and gentle creatures.
 - The tree stump fell loudly, the roots collapsed, and one thousand nine hundred and nineteen branches fell. Each branch falls into a land, a village: Sap, Giap, Bi, Lo, Ong, Sa, Vong, Khoong, De, Co Ke, Tien Lang...
 - The list of Muong stretches out like a living map: Bang, Ben, Lap, Yen, Khi, Do, En, Kho, Ai, Truong, Kha, Nat, Trao, Mo, Boi, Khoi, Kim, Oi, Nang, Lamp, Che, Men, Tre, Vam, Vong, Lut, Lo, Vong, Sieve, Tong, Bang, Un.
 - Next comes Khao, Ao, Coi, Chan, Warm, Dua, O, Gia, Rach, Met, Hao Hao, Len, Swallow, Ngon, Top, Wipe, Kho, Smoke, Nen, Khoi, Len, Vin Vong; then Vang, Vo, Trang, Bamboo, Luong Trang, Te, Vanh, Vang, Muong Chin, Muong Rong, Mat, Coc, Muong Tien, Kho, Man, Coc, Laeng, Me, Muong Man.
 - Only a young green branch still stands alone, demanding to return to the land of Thunder, the King of Heaven. The country already exists, the village already exists, but humanity does not yet exist.
- **Meaning / Storytelling highlight:** This is a "map" chapter: each si branch is a region, a community. The narrator should read the list with a steady rhythm like counting, occasionally pressing a name for the child to remember.

### Chapter 6: Giving birth
- **Central Characters & Images:** Da Dan Lady, Silver Butterfly Cun, Bo Butterfly Cun, Nang A, Nang Hai, ten fairies, Tung Bird, Tot Bird, Bich Bip bird, Peacock bird, Cao Chao Drum, Skylark Hen, Po eggs - Chieng eggs, twelve eggs hatched into human lines.
- **Detailed developments:**
 - Fallen tree: loose branches turn into Da Dan's arms and legs, ruffled branches turn into heads, loose branches turn into eyes and ears, towering branches turn into chest and back. She often wants to eat fish and rice, but her mouth speaks words of advice and her stomach thinks wise things.
 - Lady Da Dan laid two eggs: one hatched with Silver Butterfly Cun, the other with Cun Mom Bo. The two brothers ate well, grew quickly, were taller than the hills, laughed like a drum, had shoulder blades eighty feet long, and spine seven hundred feet long.
 - The King of Heaven sent his daughter down to play. Ten fairies went down to bathe in the Tam and Te rivers; Ms. A and Ms. Hai met two Cun guys who were hunting deer and deer. The two men invited them home, led the horses and brought them down.
 - Heaven's drum strikes the fifth watch, the fairies quickly fly back; Miss A and Miss Hai were left behind because the sky gate was closed. Cun Mom Bac married Nang A so his family name was Lang; Cun Mom Bo married Nang Hai so his family name was Dan.
 - The two girls gave birth to ten children: Khong Lang, Khong Va, Khong Tap – Khong Toi, Khong Em – Khong Am, Khong Lam – Khong Lo, Khong May, Khong Lot, Vang, Lang Chang Xe; The youngest are the Cock Chim Tung and the Hen Chim Tot.
 - Chim Tung and Chim Tot had no place to live and tried to swallow the leaves and hang their necks; Mrs. Da Dan advised not to take risks and showed her how to preen her feathers on the rocks of Hang Trong and Hang Hao, and dig soil to make rivers and fields. The bird learned to weave silk and brocade from her.
 - When a bird builds a nest, it lays one thousand nine hundred and nineteen good eggs, and one seven-cornered, nine-sided egg is a bad egg. Ninety days of incubation without hatching; Angry birds throw eggs into the sky, onto the ground, into the river: God of Lightning, God of Clouds, shrews, pigs, elephants, fish, wild animals, grass, cork, and pine trees hatch.
 - Next time the bird lays a black egg with four segments and twelve straps - a Breed egg, a Breed egg. Mrs. Da Dan advertised all over Heaven's haunted land to find someone to incubate. Bibip, a peacock that incubates for nine months without hatching; The Cao Chao drum and the Skylark hen know how to use golden wings to cover the rising sun, silver wings to cover the setting sun, and use fake rocks and leaves to crack the Po eggs and hatch the Chieng eggs.
 - The eggs hatch out in Lao, Kinh, Mon, Muong, Thai, Man, Meo; Dat Dat, Lang Ta Cai, Lang Cun Can, Bu Buom Khang, Xang Xi, Va Hai Kip, Miss Chin, Chu Chuong Muong Nuoc, albinos, orphans, widows, cripples, and one-blind people. Earthly people were born from Drum Cave, taking their name from Hao Cave.
- **Significance / Storytelling highlight:** This is the longest chapter in Act I, including four major beats: Lady Da Dan - two fairies - Chim Tung Chim Tot - eggs hatch. Need to rest clearly between beats; Da Dan's voice is the warm thread that connects the whole chapter.

### Chapter 7: Divide the year into the month
- **Central Characters & Images:** Mr. Cuong Minh Vang Ram, Ms. Thunder God, nine suns with twelve bright faces, Ngao family with red painted slingshots, cocks and chickens, smooth hens and ducks, Mr. Pong Peu, Mr. Thu Tha, Ms. Thu Thien, Mr. Bang Pin.
- **Detailed developments:**
 - The land, the sky, the village, the trees, the people who cook rice, the people who cast nets already exist; but there is no date yet, I don't know which date to put forward first.
 - Mr. Cuong Minh Vang Ram and Lady Thunder Troi mined copper and gold, casting nine suns and twelve bright faces. The harsh sun can't produce corn in the fields, can't produce rice in the fields, can't catch fish.
 - Chu Chuong Ban must shoot less of the sun and moon. The Ngao family had red-painted slings, black-painted crossbows, and smoked bamboo arrows that shot down eight suns and eleven bright sides, leaving one sun during the day and one bright side at night.
 - Since then there have been days and nights, but there have not been years or months. Muong advertises to find people to call on the sky to bring light, and rewards them with silver, gold, and buffaloes and cows.
 - The chicken and duck hen accepted the invitation with the following conditions: the hens had to incubate the eggs they laid, and the chicks that hatched had to be taken care of; Good season to eat badger seeds, bad season to eat flat seeds. The chicken jumped on the back of the duck, swam in the river, crowed in the East and then turned around to the West; The sun, the bright face, hears the sound and rises.
 - Mr. Thu Tha and Ms. Thu Thien stood up and decreed five months: a year with twelve months, a month with thirty days, some years are full and some years are short, months are full and months are short.
 - Name each month associated with big things: January is for silkworms to climb leaves; February and March let the fish go to the fields; April allowed King Mong Nong to dry his back; May and June let the King of the country raise soldiers; July and August for King Tai Tuong to create wind and rain; September and October give birth to fish and rice season; November and December let Mr. Bang Pin ride the clouds to the upper world.
 - Mr. Bang Pin rides black clouds, white clouds, wind and rain clouds; The rain is pouring, the rain is sinking. Under the earth, the wind roars, the forest has fortune, the Cun Buom Bac family, the Gong Vo family already have it; In the house, the old grandparents have grandchildren and descendants.
- **Meaning / Storytelling highlight:** The chapter closes Act I by completing the cosmic order: there is land, water, land, people, and then there is time. The last sentence opens Act II with the name Dit Dang, the person who will build the village and teach the people.
