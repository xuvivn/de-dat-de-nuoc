# Level 2 (Detailed Outline) — Chapter VI: The Triumph of the Copper Lead Tam Quan Ke Cho

> **Level goal:** Detailed outline of the last chapter (28) of *Giving Birth to Earth*, for storytellers 9–10 years old. This is the closing chapter of the epic, consisting of three stages: arranging clothes for the king, arranging the palanquin for the throne, and then bringing the king back to Dong Chi - ending with the affirmation that the land has been made.

- **Significance / Narrative highlight:** This is the closing chapter of the entire epic, and is the only chapter with three clear parts. The narrator needs to read in three stages: the first part is light and meticulous like a new profession; The second part is happy, there is a worker who wants to eat; The third part is slow and solemn, like a real procession. The rooster's words and the old man's words are the two quietest places - read slowly so that children can hear the idea "returning to Ke Cho but still remembering the ancestral land".
