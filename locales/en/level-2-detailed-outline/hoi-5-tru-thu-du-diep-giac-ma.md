# Level 2 (Detailed Outline) — Act V: Exorcising Beasts and Suppressing Rebellions

> **Level goal:** Detailed outline of the 4 chapters (24–27) of *Giving Birth to Earth*, for storytellers 9–10 years old. This episode is a series of battles: the monster is born from unjust blood, then the animals that eat its corpse also go crazy, and finally the battle with the brother who died and became a ghost.
