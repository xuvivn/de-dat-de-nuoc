## 3. Level 1 presentation conventions

- Chapter title:`### Chương N: Tên chương`.
- Do not add prose references; The chapter starts straight into the lyrics.
- Each verse has one line, maximum about 14 words, keep the reading natural.
- At the end of the chapter there is`#### Lưu ý giọng kể`with 3–5 bullet points.
- Long chapters are divided by bold line `00-epic-soul-overview.md`, without separating chapter numbers.

## 4. Reminiscent painting style (Level 4)

- **Material:** black charcoal combined with indigo chalk, terracotta and yellow earth on ivory paper.
- **Spirit:** Wooden hand strokes, splashes of color, evoking motifs of bronze drums, stilt roofs, forests, mountains and rivers of Muong region.
- **Rules:** no text, no labels, no digital details; One picture per chapter.

See[prompts-28-scenes.md](level-4-visual-prompts/prompts-28-scenes.md).
