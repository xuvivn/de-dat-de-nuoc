## Notes for the narrator

- **Do not read picture captions to children.** Pictures are for *looking at*, not for *reading*.
- **One picture — one landmark.** If the child is rambling, point to a detail in the picture and ask: *"What about this place?"*
- **Three paintings to keep the longest:** Picture 20 (Vang Anh's advice), Picture 21 (small ax blade), Picture 23 (two children looking at fire). These are the three landmarks that support the entire Act IV.
- **Picture 28 is the final picture.** After finishing the story, you can leave the picture alone on the screen or on the table, without saying anything else.
