#### Note the narrator's voice
- **Three parts:** This chapter is long, read into three distinct stops - taking care of the clothes, taking care of the palanquin, and bringing the king home. Each stage changes rhythm.
- **Dat Cai Dang:** Old voice, slow but clear; Asking for directions sounds like you're really lost, so the child should follow.
- **The silkworm said:** His voice was small, clear, almost whispering; This is the most beautiful part of part one.
- **Mother Da Dan:** Change your tone to be greedy, demanding, a bit excessive — children will laugh; But when sewing clothes, read carefully.
- **Giving the king:** Read slowly and solemnly; Then the king told the old man to lower his voice warmly.
- **Conclusion:** The last six sentences are read slowly and evenly, then pop up at "Bright country, bright water!" — remained silent for a beat after that.
