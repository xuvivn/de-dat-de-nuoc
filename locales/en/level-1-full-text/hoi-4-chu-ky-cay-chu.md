#### Note the narrator's voice
- **A group of ants:** The voice is arrogant and posh, like someone attending a big festival; “We are still inferior to us by one sky and one earth” read full of challenge.
- **Three brothers:** Three different voices — Cun Toi is hot, Cun Tang is moderate, Cun Khuong is cold and calculating. Keep those three voices throughout the chapter.
- **The wrestling match with the Noong Couple:** Read quickly, with a rhythm of counting one, two... like a wrestling drum; This is where the children laugh the loudest in Act IV.
- **The cypress tree appears:** Lower your voice very low and say: "It certainly feels majestic" - read slowly, solemnly, as if standing in front of a sacred object.
- **Oriole:** Small, clear, urgent voice; Its instructions must be read clearly, because the next chapter will remember these words.
- **Conclusion:** The last four sentences are read gradually, like footsteps searching for a friend.
